Under the hood

Open models. Plain algorithms. Cloudinary for every photo.

  • Gemma 4 (open-weight) understands free text and photos — hosted on the Gemini API, or fully offline on the hospital's own machine through Ollama.
  • Cloudinary turns a plain staff photo into a direction sign in one URL: e_blur_faces, e_improve, the step's arrow and a Hindi + English line as text layers, then f_auto,q_auto for 2G.
  • Dijkstra over a corridor graph picks the true shortest path; the model only ever picks a room id, which is validated.
  • Photo → map: a building is traced from one photo of its fire-evacuation plan.
  • Three.js 3D view, a service worker for offline use, and the browser's own speech APIs.
